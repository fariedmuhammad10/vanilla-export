3. Export Packaging Standard

  • • Primary (Inner): Food-grade high-barrier PA/PE nylon vacuum-sealed pouches (1 kg, 2.5 kg, or 5 kg net per pouch).
  • • Secondary (Outer): Double-wall corrugated export master carton (10 kg, 20 kg, or 25 kg net) with inner moisture liner.
  • • Traceability Labeling: Batch/lot code, crop year, grade, net/gross weight, HS code, and country of origin printed per pouch & carton.

4. Storage & Shelf Life

  • • Shelf Life: 24 months from packing date when stored unopened in original factory vacuum seal.
  • • Storage Conditions: Cool, dark, dry ambient warehouse (15°C – 22°C / 59°F – 72°F, RH < 65%). Do not refrigerate or freeze.
  • • Post-Opening Care: Reseal opened bags immediately in airtight food-grade barrier containers or wrap tightly in wax/parchment paper.
