9. Packing, Documents & Terms

  • Packing: new food-grade PP woven or jute bags with PE liner, 25–50 kg net; smaller packs on request. Lot number, grade, origin and harvest period on every bag.
  • Documents: Phytosanitary Certificate (Indonesian Quarantine Agency), Certificate of Origin, Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Bill of Lading / Air Waybill, lab CoA for EU/US orders. FDA Prior Notice filed for US shipments.
  • Lot sizes: regular lots by sea (indicatively from 500 kg–1 MT per product, mixed spices can share one LCL shipment); small trial lots by air on request at a higher per-kg cost.
  • Incoterms & payment: FOB Jakarta (Tanjung Priok / CGK) or CIF/CIP. New buyers: 50% advance, balance before shipment against documents.
  • Samples: 50–250 g per grade. The sample itself is free; courier on your account, or prepaid and credited in full against your first order.

Specification basis: Codex CXS 326-2017 (black & white pepper); Codex proposed draft standard for nutmeg; SNI 0004:2013 & 0005:2013 (white & black pepper); ESA Quality Minima (cassia, nutmeg); ASTA Cleanliness Specifications; AGMARK grade standards (cloves, mace); EU Regulation 2023/915 (contaminants).
