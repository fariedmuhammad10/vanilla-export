Procurement FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions by Importers

1. How can our R&D or culinary team request an evaluation sample (50g–100g)? ▼

We provide 50g–100g evaluation sample packs (vacuum-sealed Grade A or Extraction Grade A/B pods) dispatched via DHL Express or FedEx. Following standard B2B trade practice, evaluation samples are complimentary while international courier postage is covered by the buyer (either via your DHL/FedEx account number or prepaid courier invoice).

2. How do you prevent mold and aroma loss during international air freight? ▼

Mold in vanilla shipments is caused by premature packaging before water activity equalizes. Our pods undergo a full 4-month sun-curing and wooden-chest conditioning cycle first. Prior to dispatch, every pod is hand-inspected on stainless steel tables and sealed inside high-barrier food-grade nylon vacuum pouches, then packed in double-wall corrugated cartons.

3. Which grade is more cost-effective for industrial vanilla extract manufacturing? ▼

Extraction Grade A/B (18%–25% moisture) is purpose-built for extract makers and flavor houses. Because the moisture content is lower than Gourmet Grade A (28%–35%), you receive more dry vanilla solid mass per kilogram purchased—maximizing vanillin and aromatic ester yield during alcohol percolation or oleoresin extraction.

4. What export documents accompany each air cargo shipment? ▼

Every commercial shipment under HS Code 0905.10.00 is accompanied by an official Phytosanitary Certificate issued by the Indonesian Agricultural Quarantine Agency, Certificate of Origin (CoO), Commercial Invoice, Packing List, and official airline Air Waybill (AWB). Third-party lab COA (vanillin & microbiology) can also be arranged upon buyer request.
